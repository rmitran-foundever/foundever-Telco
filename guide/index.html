Telecom leaders guide

Beat telecom support's three biggest challenges

Telecom teams are stuck with high attrition, rising AI investment with no clear ROI, and persistent customer churn — while struggling to differentiate. This guide shows you how to tackle these challenges, so you can focus on what really matters: guiding strategy and driving growth.

What you'll learn

01

Cost and AI pressure arrived on the same day

Cost and AI are tied exactly at 54% for telcos' top challenge. Neither is ahead of the other, and neither can wait for the other — so investment and operating model decisions have to pass both tests at once.

02

Customer churn has its own gravity

Over a third call it a top challenge in a market so saturated and price-driven that leaders rate it 3.5 out of 5 in complexity — a problem in its own right, not just fallout from cost and AI pressure.

03

The starkest blind spot in this whole guide

Nearly half of telcos name agent attrition as their #1 complaint, yet only 15% see it as a genuine business risk. It ranks dead last, 11th of 13, in what buyers screen for in a new partner.

04

Outage-ready surge operations

Well over half of the respondents mention CPNI as their top regulatory pressure. The true test is what happens when a network event hits and call volume spikes all at once.

The takeaway

Treat attrition as a screening question, not a permanent complaint.

Telcos have connected cost and AI investment tightly, and they're ahead on churn prediction. The next move is the same discipline that turns outage response from a fire drill into a competitive edge — applied to agent attrition.

What to look for in a partner

  • Certifies agents specifically on carrier systems, backed by intelligent copilots instead of generic scripts
  • Personalizes retention outreach using customer journey mapping and historical data
  • Backs agent retention with structural changes, flexible schedules, quality-based incentives
  • Activates surge-ready, CPNI-compliant operations the moment a network event hits
